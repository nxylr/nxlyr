## By ear (founder's notes)

Turn taking:
- No cut-off during the 0.7 s pause on 9 of 10 calls. On one call the bot started speaking during a pause of over 1.5 s. That is the expected trade-off of the 0.8 s backstop (about 1.0 s of silence); the knob is NXLYR_TURN_BACKSTOP_SECS. Watch it on real callers.
- With the phone on speaker, a cough stopped the bot; with the phone at the ear, coughing did not. Quiet room, fan running. Watch item.

What the bot says (all prompt v2 unless noted):
- Echoes one-word answers: the caller says "yes", the bot answers with an awkward "Yes".
- Filler openers make it sound like an AI: "Great", "I see", "Sure thing", "Absolutely", "Sure", "I hear you".
- Repeats the project's full name every time instead of "the project" or "it".
- The opening says it is calling about a query the caller sent, without saying who it is or which company it is calling from (the greeting text, in code).
- Lists every fact it knows, then ends with a question; no back and forth.
- The 25-25-25-25 payment split sounds robotic read out like that (prompt and knowledge-base wording).
- Asking for a site visit sometimes makes it wrap up and end the call. Consistent with the events log: book_site_visit fired on 10 calls but transfer_to_human only on 9, as in the baseline (10 and 9). Needs checking before prompt v2 and task 008 are specced.
- "Meridian Heights" was heard as something else and the bot said "Eridian Heights" (STT; candidate fix: project names as Deepgram key terms, plus a prompt rule to confirm a name it is unsure of).
- Still struggles with "Rs 12 thousand" (open: hearing or speaking it).
